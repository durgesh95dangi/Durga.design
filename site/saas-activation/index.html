The problem

Signups are fine. Activation isn’t.

Think of a gym that sells memberships in January and never sees most people again by March. The signup worked. The habit never formed. Trials work the same way.

01

Signups are fine, activation isn’t.

People create accounts, look around once, and never come back to do the one thing that makes the product useful.

02

Users stall at setup.

Connecting data, inviting the team, picking settings. Each step is a place to quit, and most people quietly do.

03

Trials end before anyone sees value.

The trial clock runs out while users are still figuring out where to start, so the paywall feels like a surprise.

FAQ

Before you book.

What timezone do you work in?

I work US mornings. My evening in India overlaps the US East Coast morning, so our calls happen in your working day.

How does the process work?

Start with a free teardown or a short call. Then a 5-day Audit finds the biggest leaks. If the fixes make sense, a 3-week Sprint redesigns the flow. A monthly retainer is optional after launch, to run experiments and measure the lift.

What tools do you use?

Figma for design and prototypes, Loom for async updates, and email or a shared Slack channel for day-to-day questions.

How does payment work?

The Audit is paid 100% upfront. The Sprint is 50% to start and 50% on handoff. The retainer is billed monthly in advance. The full $499 Audit fee is credited if you book the Sprint within 14 days.

What do you need from us?

A test account for your product, your analytics if you have them, the activation metric you care about, and one point of contact.

Do you build it too?

No. I design the flow and hand it off with a Figma file, a clickable prototype and a Loom walkthrough. I answer developer questions for 14 days after handoff.
