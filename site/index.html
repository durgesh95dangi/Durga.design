About me

I design the hard parts of software: the screens people use every day, and the ones they have to trust.

Think of a busy bank counter. If the form is confusing, the queue grows and people get frustrated. Software works the same way, only the queue is invisible. That’s where I spend my time: dense dashboards, multi-step flows, money moving between accounts. I work closely with product managers and engineers, test ideas with real users, and hand off specs developers can build without guessing.
