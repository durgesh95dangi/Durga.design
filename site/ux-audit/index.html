The hard truth

Every drop-off is a tax on your revenue.

You’ve built a great product and you’re paying for traffic. People land, scroll for five seconds, get confused by the layout and bounce. Or they start signing up, hit unnecessary friction, and leave.

You can’t out-market a bad user experience. If the funnel leaks, more traffic is water poured into a bucket with a hole in it. Nine times out of ten, the problem is cognitive friction:

  • Your primary CTA is hidden below the fold.
  • Your pricing page creates choice paralysis.
  • Your onboarding flow asks for too much information too early.
  • Your mobile experience is broken.

These are silent killers. They don’t throw error codes. They just quietly kill your conversion rate.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just a link to your product, any test credentials needed to log in, and a brief description of who your target user is. Analytics access (Mixpanel, GA4) is a bonus, but not required.

How is this different from a normal UX design project?

A normal UX project takes months and costs $10k+. This is a surgical strike: fast, affordable, and focused on high-impact conversion levers rather than reinventing your entire design system.

Do you also write the code?

No. I provide the design solutions, wireframes and UX logic. Your developers implement the changes, and the roadmap is structured so they can execute without ambiguity.

What exactly is included in the $499 UX audit?

A screen-by-screen video teardown of your product, low-fidelity wireframe redesigns in Figma for your core screens, a prioritised dev roadmap in Notion graded by impact vs. effort, and a 30-minute strategy call to go through it.

How long does the UX audit take?

You get the full audit within 36 hours of sending your product link and any login details I need.

What if the audit doesn’t help?

It comes with a 100% money-back guarantee. If it doesn’t give you clear, useful fixes, you get your money back.

How is a UX audit different from usability testing?

An audit is an expert review of your product against proven usability principles. Usability testing watches real users try tasks. An audit is faster and cheaper and finds most of the obvious friction; testing shows behaviour nobody predicted. They work well together.

Who is the UX audit for?

SaaS and fintech founders and product teams who already get traffic or signups but lose people in onboarding, pricing or core workflows, and want to know exactly what to fix first.
