FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you work with fintech or SaaS startups?

Yes. I specialize in B2B SaaS, enterprise, and fintech. If your product has complex workflows and heavy data, I'm your guy.

Are you available for design consulting or contract work?

Yes. I do everything from two-week UX audits to massive multi-month redesigns. Just tell me what's broken.

What's your design process like?

I focus on business logic first. Then I prototype in Figma and hand off pixel-perfect specs with Loom videos. Your engineers won't have to guess.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I'm based in Bengaluru but work mostly with US and European startups. It's fully async. I use Figma, Notion, and Loom to move fast without endless meetings.

What industries do you have experience in?

Fintech, B2B SaaS, and Enterprise. I also have deep roots in Agri-tech and Field Service Management. Complex industries are where I thrive.
