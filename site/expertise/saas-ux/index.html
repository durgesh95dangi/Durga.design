Context

Why it matters.

Enterprise SaaS fails when users need training manuals to complete daily tasks. I design streamlined permission structures, contextual sidebars, keyboard-driven navigation, and zero-state onboarding flows that transform complex software into competitive moats.

Bottlenecks

Friction I solve.

  • Mitigating Friction Debt across complex multi-step user onboarding flows
  • Designing granular RBAC (Role-Based Access Control) without cluttering navigational chrome
  • Structuring multi-tenant workspaces with intuitive account-switching ergonomics
  • Turning empty states from 'ghost towns' into interactive value accelerators

Principles

Rules I design by.

  1. Never make a user answer a question before delivering value.
  2. Keyboard shortcuts for power users; progressive disclosure for novices.
  3. High-density data layouts must always prioritize glanceability over decoration.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do you measure success in B2B SaaS UX?

We track Product Qualified Lead (PQL) activation rate, Time-to-Value (TTV), feature adoption depth, ticket volume submitted to support for workflow confusion, and net revenue retention.

How do you handle technical debt while redesigning a SaaS app?

We decouple UX redesign into modular workflow sprints. Rather than a risky complete rewrite, we overhaul the most painful funnel leaks (such as onboarding and main dashboard views) incrementally.
