Overview

What this engagement is.

Skip the overhead, slow hiring, and salary expenses of a full-time senior designer or slow agency contracts. Get unlimited design requests delivered one at a time with rapid 24-48 hour turnarounds, daily async communication, and zero long-term commitments.

  • Teams that need the flexibility to pause design spend during slow development months

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does the pause feature work?

Billing cycles are based on a 31-day period. If you use 20 days and pause, you have 11 remaining days banked to use whenever your next sprint starts.

How many requests can I submit at once?

You can add unlimited requests to your queue. They will be actively designed and delivered one by one in your chosen priority sequence.
