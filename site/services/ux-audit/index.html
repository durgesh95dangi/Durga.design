Overview

What this engagement is.

A comprehensive, no-fluff teardown of your critical conversion paths, onboarding friction, and core product workflows. You receive a recorded Loom video walkthrough, prioritized heuristic scorecard, and actionable Figma wireframe fixes.

Who it’s for

Ideal if you are…

  • Post-revenue SaaS & Fintech platforms with unexplained user churn
  • Founders pouring capital into ads that fail to convert on landing or signup
  • Teams preparing for a major funding round or growth sprint

What you get

Delivered to you.

  • 45+ minute screen-by-screen Loom video walkthrough
  • Prioritized heuristic evaluation spreadsheet (Impact vs. Effort)
  • Production-ready Figma wireframes solving top 3 friction leaks
  • 30-minute 1-on-1 strategy call to review implementation roadmap

Outcomes

What changes.

  • Immediate clarity on why users bounce during onboarding
  • Engineering-ready blueprints that developers can execute immediately
  • Backed by a 100% 30-day money-back satisfaction guarantee

Process

How it works.

Access & Context

You share test credentials, target user profiles, and product analytics.

Heuristic Teardown

I test every screen through the lens of cognitive load, interaction cost, and mental models.

Figma Fixes & Video

I redesign the broken screens in Figma and record a comprehensive breakdown.

Debrief Call

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What do you need from my team to get started?

A link to your staging or production app, test login credentials, and a quick summary of your target ICP and core conversion goal.

Do my developers have to rewrite the entire app?

No. The audit specifically identifies high-impact, low-effort changes (microcopy, button placement, default states, form simplification) that can be deployed quickly.
