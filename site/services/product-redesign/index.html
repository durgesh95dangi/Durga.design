Legacy Product Redesign & Modernization

Overview

What this engagement is.

Legacy software often accumulates years of disjointed features, outdated design paradigms, and confusing navigation. I surgically restructure the interface without alienating power users, modernizing the visual layer while dramatically increasing operational velocity.

Who it’s for

Ideal if you are…

  • Platforms suffering from customer churn citing 'clunky or confusing' interfaces
  • Products built by engineers that have outgrown their initial quick UI
  • Companies undergoing rebranding or expanding into enterprise sales tiers

What you get

Delivered to you.

  • Full Product UX Heuristic Evaluation & Pain-Point Map
  • Consolidated Navigation & Layout Restructure
  • Complete Modernized UI Screen Suite across Desktop & Mobile
  • Component Migration Strategy to minimize frontend engineering friction

Outcomes

What changes.

  • Immediate decrease in customer support onboarding and navigation tickets
  • Renewed customer delight and positive sentiment across review platforms
  • Higher conversion from trial to paid accounts

Process

How it works.

Current State Audit

Pinpoint every bottleneck causing user frustration and support tickets.

Workflow Simplification

Consolidate redundant screens, reduce click counts, and streamline data entry.

Modern Design Overhaul

Introduce clean typography, balanced contrast, and modern design tokens.

Phased Rollout Support

Support engineering through beta testing, user feedback, and final launch.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do we prevent power users from rebelling against changes?

We preserve keyboard shortcuts, respect existing mental models, and roll out improvements progressively with clear in-app change highlights.
