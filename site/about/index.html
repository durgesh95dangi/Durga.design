I’m Durga Prasad, a freelance product designer with 8+ years helping early-stage and scale-up tech companies turn complex engineering into intuitive, commercially defensible products.

Most software doesn’t fail for lack of engineering horsepower. It fails because of friction debt: convoluted onboarding, ambiguous navigation, and cognitive overload that make users churn before they see value.

I bridge user psychology, tokenised design systems and front-end engineering to remove that friction, whether it’s a fintech lending flow, an AI observability dashboard, or a multi-tenant SaaS platform.

Design philosophy

How I approach software.

Designing software is an engineering discipline anchored in behavioural economics and ergonomics, not decoration.

01

Systems over isolated screens

Single mockups don't scale. I build tokenised design systems where every Figma variable maps 1:1 to code, so what ships matches what was designed.

02

Time-to-value is everything

If users cannot feel value within 180 seconds, churn spikes. I design progressive onboarding that postpones friction until after value is unlocked.

03

Data density without chaos

B2B and fintech operators need actionable data, not empty white space. I design glanceable hierarchies, sticky table geometry and contextual drill-downs.

04

Engineering empathy by default

I speak TypeScript, Git and React. Designs ship with edge cases, loading states and accessible states, so handoff has no guesswork.
